## Recipe for Cassava mit Chilli

## Ingredients
  - Cassava
  - Oil
  - Spicy Chilli
  - Onion
  - Hot spices
  - Unparalleled spice tolerance

## Instructions
  - Boil Cassava
  - Crush the chilli, spices, onion into a hot paste
  - Eat as you will die tommorrow! (you might if you don't have tolerance)
