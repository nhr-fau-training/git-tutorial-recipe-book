# Guacamole recipe updated niname



## Ingredients

* 500 avocados
* 1 chili
* 1 lime
* 1 tsp salt
* 1/2 onion
* 1 tomato


## Instructions

* Take out the avocado and put it on a bowl
* Chop in little pices the onion and tomato
* Put the onion and tomato in the bowl
* squeeze lime over the mix
* add salt
* and mix well
* enjoy!
